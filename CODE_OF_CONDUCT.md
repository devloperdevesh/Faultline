
# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

Faultline is an open-source project dedicated to building better security remediation workflows. We are committed to creating a respectful, inclusive, and constructive environment for everyone who participates in the community.

We welcome contributions from people of all experience levels, backgrounds, and skill sets.

## Community Standards

| Expected Behaviour | What It Means |
|---|---|
| Respect | Treat everyone with courtesy and professionalism. |
| Constructive Feedback | Critique ideas, code, and technical decisions—not people. |
| Inclusivity | Welcome different perspectives, backgrounds, and experience levels. |
| Collaboration | Help others learn, improve, and contribute effectively. |
| Accountability | Accept feedback gracefully and take responsibility for mistakes. |
| Professionalism | Keep discussions focused, helpful, and relevant to the project. |

## Unacceptable Behaviour

The following behaviours are not acceptable:

| Behaviour | Examples |
|---|---|
| Harassment | Intimidation, threats, or unwanted personal attention. |
| Discrimination | Abusive or exclusionary conduct based on personal characteristics. |
| Personal Attacks | Insults, humiliation, or hostile comments directed at individuals. |
| Disruptive Conduct | Repeated trolling, deliberate disruption, or bad-faith participation. |
| Privacy Violations | Publishing private information without permission. |
| Abusive Communication | Hate speech, discriminatory language, or persistent harassment. |

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all Faultline community spaces, including:

- GitHub issues and pull requests
- Discussions and code reviews
- Project-related communication channels
- Community events and other spaces where individuals represent Faultline

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, report it through the contact method documented in the repository's `SECURITY.md` or another designated maintainer contact.

Please include relevant context and links when possible. Do not publish sensitive personal information in public issues.

Reports will be reviewed by the project maintainers as appropriate, with respect for the privacy of everyone involved.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards.

Depending on the severity and frequency of a violation, actions may include:

1. A warning and request to correct the behaviour.
2. Temporary restrictions on participation.
3. Removal of inappropriate content.
4. Temporary or permanent exclusion from project spaces.

Maintainers will consider the nature and impact of each incident when determining an appropriate response.

## Maintainer Responsibilities

Maintainers are expected to apply these standards consistently, communicate decisions respectfully, and prioritize the safety and well-being of the community.

## Acknowledgment

By participating in the Faultline community, you agree to follow these standards and help maintain a respectful, constructive, and inclusive environment.
